# USER MANUAL & OPERATIONAL GUIDE
## Team Padua Portal — Financial Advisory Operations & Client Servicing System

**Target Audience:** Financial Advisors, Operations Staff, Administrative Personnel, Team Members  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-11  

---

## 1. Welcome to Team Padua Portal

The **Team Padua Portal** is your central web workspace for managing client prospects, submitting insurance policy change requests, generating official Sun Life policy servicing documents, tracking attendance, and staying updated with team announcements.

---

## 2. Getting Started & Logging In

### 2.1 Accessing the Portal
1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari recommended).
2. Navigate to the portal web address (e.g., `https://portal.teampadua.com` or `http://localhost:3000`).

### 2.2 Signing In
1. On the Login screen, enter your registered **Email address** and **Password**.
2. Click **Sign In**.
3. Alternatively, if your organization uses Google accounts, click **Continue with Google**.

> [!NOTE]
> **New User Account Approval:** If you recently registered a new account, your status will initially be **Pending**. An Administrator must approve your account before you can log in.

---

## 3. Navigating the Portal

### 3.1 Navigating as a Financial Advisor / Team Member
After logging in, you will land on your **User Personal Dashboard**:
- **Navigation Sidebar (Left):** Access Dashboard, Attendance, Calendar, Messages, FAQ, Portals, and Settings.
- **Top Header Bar:** Search bar (global search across clients and tasks), quick notifications bell, and user profile avatar.

---

## 4. Core Features & Step-by-Step Instructions

### 4.1 Client Prospect Servicing Tracker (CPST)
*Location: Sidebar → Client Servicing → CPST*

1. **Viewing Clients:** Scroll or search through the client list to view client details, relationship status, birthdate, and policy notes.
2. **Adding a New Client:** Click the **+ New Client** button, complete the client form (Name, Birthdate, Status: `Prospect` / `Lead` / `Serviced`), and click **Save**.
3. **Tracking Birthdays:** Upcoming client birthdays are automatically highlighted in the **Birthday Milestone Card** on your dashboard.

---

### 4.2 Submitting Client Servicing Requests & Generating PDFs

You can submit policy servicing requests for the following change types:

| Module | Purpose | PDF Form Generated |
|---|---|---|
| **ACR** | Change assigned Financial Advisor | Advisor Change Request Form |
| **BCR (CPC)** | Change policy beneficiaries | Beneficiary Change Request Form |
| **ACICR** | Update client address & phone | Address & Contact Info Change Request |
| **FST** | Switch investment funds | Fund Switching Request Form |
| **FWR (MNGT)** | Withdraw policy funds | Fund Withdrawal Request Form |
| **ACA (PPU)** | Update billing & policy details | Auto Change Arrangement Form |

#### How to Submit a Request & Download PDF:
1. Navigate to the desired module under **Client Servicing** (e.g., `ACR` or `BCR`).
2. Click **+ New Request**.
3. Fill out the request form (select client, policy number, details).
4. Draw or upload the required **Digital Signature** in the signature box.
5. Click **Save Request**.
6. Click **Download PDF** to generate and save the official filled Sun Life servicing PDF onto your computer.

---

### 4.3 Daily Attendance Tracking
*Location: Sidebar → Attendance*

1. To log your arrival, click **Time In**.
2. To record lunch or breaks, click **Break Out** and **Break In**.
3. At the end of your workday, click **Time Out**.
4. Your total daily hours will automatically calculate and save to your monthly attendance record.

---

### 4.4 Personal To-Do & Tasks
*Location: Dashboard → To-Do Checklist*

1. Type a task title in the **Add a new task...** field and press **Enter**.
2. Click the checkbox next to a task when completed.
3. Click the trash icon to remove completed tasks.

---

## 5. Account Settings & Password Reset

### 5.1 Resetting Your Password (If Forgotten)
1. On the Login page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the link sent to your email inbox to choose a new password.

> [!SECURITY NOTE]
> **Password Policy:** Your new password must be at least 8 characters long and cannot be one of your last 5 used passwords.
